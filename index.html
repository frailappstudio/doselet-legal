Tabs: Medicine Tracker

Support

Tabs helps you log every dose in a couple of taps and see how many medicine days you've had this month.

Need help? Email frailappstudio@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version. We read every message.

Common questions

How do I bring in my medicines from Apple Health? On iOS 26 or later, tap Import from Apple Health while setting up, then choose the medicines to share. Tabs reads them and the doses you marked as taken. To change what Tabs can see, open the Health app > your profile > Apps > Tabs.

Do doses I log in Tabs show up in Apple Health? Not yet. Apple's medications feature only lets apps read, not write.

Is Tabs medical advice? No. Tabs is a tracking tool, not a medical device. The monthly limits come from the International Classification of Headache Disorders (ICHD-3). Talk to your doctor or pharmacist about what's right for you.

How do I cancel Tabs Pro? iOS Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Tabs. Cancelling keeps Pro until the end of the period you paid for. Your data stays on your iPhone and nothing is deleted. Export is part of Tabs Pro.

How does billing work? Tabs Pro is billed through your Apple ID. Payment is charged at confirmation. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends. Lifetime is a one-time purchase.

I reinstalled and Pro is gone. Open Tabs and tap Restore on the Pro screen while signed in to the same Apple ID.

I reinstalled and my doses are gone. Tabs keeps your data only on your iPhone, with no cloud copy. Use Export in Settings to keep a backup file.
