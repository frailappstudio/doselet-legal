Tabs: Medicine Tracker

Privacy Policy

Effective November 26, 2026

The short version. Your medicines and doses stay on your iPhone. There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, nothing is uploaded to us or to iCloud, and we don't sell anything.

Tabs is made by Frail App Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the Tabs app does with information.

Your medicines and doses

The medicines you add, the doses you log, reasons, notes, "Did it help?" answers, reminders and settings are saved only on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else, and they are not stored in iCloud.

Apple Health

If you choose to, Tabs reads from Apple Health only the medicines you select in Apple's sharing screen and the doses you marked as taken for them. Tabs does not write to Apple Health. Health information is used only to show your medicines and history inside the app. It is saved only on your iPhone, never sent anywhere, never used for advertising or data mining, and never shared with or sold to anyone. You can change or remove Tabs's access any time in the Health app > your profile > Apps > Tabs.

Medicine information

Medicine names, classes and interaction notes come from a list built into the app, based on public sources such as FDA labels and RxNorm. Looking a medicine up never sends anything from your iPhone.

Notifications

If you allow them, Tabs sends reminders for daily medicines and a "Did it help?" check-in after a dose. These are created on your device. Nothing is sent from a server.

Purchases

Tabs Pro purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your name, email or payment details. See Apple's privacy policy.

Widgets, Siri and Shortcuts

Tabs's widgets, Control Center control, Siri and Shortcuts actions read and write the same on-device data as the app, through storage shared between the app and its widgets on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to us. If you use Siri, Apple handles your voice request under Apple's privacy policy.

Export and doctor report

Tabs Pro can create a doctor report (PDF) and a CSV file of your doses. The file is created on your iPhone. Where it goes next (Files, email, your doctor) is up to you; we never see it.

What we don't do

  • No ads and no ad networks.
  • No analytics or crash reporting tools that see your information.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps and websites.
  • No selling or renting of information.
  • No accounts, emails or phone numbers collected.

Not medical advice

Tabs is a tracking tool, not a medical device. It doesn't give medical advice.

Keeping and deleting your data

Everything Tabs stores is on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes it. Removing Tabs's access in Apple Health stops any further reading.

Children

Tabs is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above, and for big changes we'll tell you in the app.
